Add GROUPBY, PIVOTBY, and PERCENTOF to the full engine. Summaries support multiple fields, headers, filters, totals, subtotals, field and value sorting, and single built-in or LAMBDA aggregators. Vectors of aggregator functions are not supported. Grouping and output use the existing spill and work limits. The default engine does not include these functions.
