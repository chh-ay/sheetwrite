In a `general` column, the editor opens a time on the clock of the column's `numberLocale`, as Excel's formula bar does: a typed `9:30 PM` opens as `9:30:00 PM` in `en-US` (the default), not `21:30:00`, and as `21:30:00` in `de-DE` or `en-GB`. Find with Look in: Formulas matches the same text.
