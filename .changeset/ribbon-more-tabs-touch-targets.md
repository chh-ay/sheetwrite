The ribbon no longer cuts a tab label off when the tabs do not fit, as on a 390 px phone. The last tabs move into a **More tabs** menu instead, and picking one brings it into the row. On touch screens (`pointer: coarse`), the ribbon's minimize button, split-button arrows such as Paste options, the sheet tabs with their options and add buttons, and dashboard slicer buttons with Clear are now at least 24 by 24 px (WCAG 2.2 target size). The ribbon and sheet tab bar keep their heights, and mouse layouts keep their density.
