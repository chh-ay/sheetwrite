A ribbon button never shrinks to an icon without its label. As the ribbon narrows, a group's large buttons become small ones with their labels beside them, and a group that still does not fit collapses into one button with the group's icon and name (such as **Show/Hide**), whose dropdown lists its commands in one column with their labels. Groups collapse in the same order as before. If the collapsed groups still do not fit, the last ones move into one **More** button at the end of the row instead of being cut off. The compact ribbon's gear now shows its name, **Shortcuts**, beside the icon. Running a command from a ribbon dropdown closes the dropdown, as in Excel; focus goes where the command leaves it, such as a panel it opens, or else to the grid.
