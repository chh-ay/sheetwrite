Add the LINEST, LOGEST, TREND, GROWTH, and FREQUENCY array functions to the full engine. Regression supports more than one predictor, the `const` argument, five rows of fit statistics, and removal of collinear predictors. With `const=FALSE`, linear fits use an intercept of zero and exponential fits use a multiplier of one. Add PROB and the legacy PERCENTILE, PERCENTRANK, QUARTILE, and RANK names. The default engine does not change.
