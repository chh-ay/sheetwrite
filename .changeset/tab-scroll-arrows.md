When the sheet tabs do not fit their bar, scroll arrows show at its edges, as in Excel. Each arrow brings the next hidden sheet into view, and the one at the end reached is dimmed. A right-click on either, a long press on a touch screen, or the context menu key (Shift+F10) opens a list of every sheet; choosing one activates it. The arrows have accessible names and tooltips and are at least 24 px, in the shell and in the grid's own tab bar.
