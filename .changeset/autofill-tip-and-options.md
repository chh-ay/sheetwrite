The fill handle shows the value the last cell will get in a tip while it is dragged, and after a fill the Auto Fill Options button redoes it as Copy Cells, Fill Series, Fill Formatting Only or Fill Without Formatting (and Fill Days, Weekdays, Months or Years for dates), as one undo step, as in Excel. New glyph `autoFillOptions`.
