The cell editor keeps the cell's alignment and font while it edits, as Excel's in-cell editor does: right-aligned and centred text stays in place, and the cell's font family, size, bold, italic, colour, underline and strikethrough show in the editor. Under General alignment a number edits at the right, and a formula or a newly typed entry at the left.
