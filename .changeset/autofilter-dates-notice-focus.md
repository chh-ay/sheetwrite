The AutoFilter list of a column that is mostly dates groups only the numbers that show as dates by year, month and day; other numbers list as they show (`15%`, `$4.50`, `7`), as in Excel. Before, they showed under the years 1899 and 1900. After **Show them** in a cut notice, the grid keeps the keyboard, so Ctrl+Z undoes it.
